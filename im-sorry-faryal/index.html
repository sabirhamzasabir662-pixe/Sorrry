I know I hurt you

I am sorry, Faryal.

I hurt you with my words and did not listen when you needed me most. There is no excuse for that. None. What I did was wrong, and I own it completely. I am not writing this to defend myself or to ask you to forget — I am writing this because you deserve to hear the truth from me:

“You came to me with your heart open, and I let you down.
I am deeply, sincerely sorry.”
